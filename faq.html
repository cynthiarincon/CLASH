Game Rules

How to Play

This game is a fun twist on Rock–Paper–Scissors. Your choices are:

  • Smash
  • Wrap
  • Slice

Winning Conditions

  • Smash beats Slice
  • Slice beats Wrap
  • Wrap beats Smash

How a Round Works

  1. Type “smash”, “wrap”, or “slice”.
  2. Click CLASH!
  3. The computer picks randomly.
  4. The winner is announced instantly!

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What can I enter?

You can type: smash, wrap, or slice.

❓ Does capitalization matter?

No — Smash, smash, or SMASH all work.

❓ What if I type something wrong?

The game will reject the input and ask you to try again.

❓ What does “RANDOMIZE” do?

It instantly picks one of the three choices for you.

❓ Is the computer fair?

Yes, it always selects randomly.
